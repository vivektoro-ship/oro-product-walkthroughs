Every screen this walkthrough looks for, the Figma frame it comes from, and its node id. Frames are named platform/gold-loan/release-visit/module/screen--state, with a handful named ad hoc (View details, Release Gold Items). Rows in red have no node id, meaning the screen has not been designed yet.

The four sections hold 116 top-level children: 5 annotation groups and 111 screen frames. Those 111 frames resolve to 79 distinct screens - 88 are wired below and the other 23 are duplicate placements, either the same frame dropped twice or a source-library copy sitting beside the screen it feeds (the px-app/onboarding/… OTP and liveliness frames in the customer section are byte-identical to the cx-app ones next to them). Nine of the 88 wired frames are themselves byte-identical pairs, because the designer reused one frame across both paths - the handover photo, visit-complete and waiting-for-receipt screens are the same file in the regular and third party sections.

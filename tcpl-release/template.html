Tenmark Capital · Gold loan release

Release visit revamp walkthrough

How a closed Tenmark gold loan is released, screen by screen, in the Oromoney customer app and the Tenmark Partner App. It covers both release paths: a normal release, where the customer collects in person, and a third-party release, where someone collects on the customer’s behalf.

Overview

The journey at a glance

The first stages are the same for both paths. The flow forks at release path selection, and both paths rejoin at gold verification and handover.

Stage 1

Close and book

The loan moves to Loan closed – release pending. The customer pays the closure amount and books a slot at the fixed Tenmark branch.

Customer app
Stage 2

Pickup

A partner opens the visit from My Visits and self-assigns it. The visit is locked to that partner for its lifetime.

Partner app
Stage 3

Path and verification

The partner selects Regular or Third party. Regular uses face match and geolocation; third party uses geolocation only.

Partner app
Stage 4

Gold verification

Every item is photographed and marked matched or not matched against the valuation record. Any mismatch blocks the release.

Partner app
Stage 5

eSign

Normal release: the customer signs the release document in their app. Third party: the collector signs the declaration before approval.

Customer appPartner app
Stage 6

Handover

A release proof photo of the person holding the gold is mandatory. It is the proof of receipt and appears in the customer app.

Partner appCustomer app

Normal release

  1. Select Regular release
  2. Customer live photo: face match and geolocation
  3. Release type: Full Release
  4. Gold verification, item by item
  5. Customer eSigns in the Oromoney app
  6. Release proof photo and handover

Third-party release

  1. Select Third party release
  2. Third party live photo: geolocation only
  3. Third party details, email OTP and DigiLocker
  4. Reason for release and its documents
  5. Collector eSigns the declaration, then submit
  6. Tenmark approves in CORE (or rejects with a reason)
  7. Release type, gold verification, handover
Chronological

End to end

Both apps in the order things happen. Each screen is tagged with the app it belongs to.

Oromoney

Customer app

Every screen the customer sees, from loan closure to gold released, including rejection, blocked and cancelled states.

Tenmark

Partner app

Every screen the Tenmark partner uses at the branch, with failure and edge states placed where they occur.

Tenmark

Admin · Tenmark CORE

The web console where Tenmark tracks every release visit, reviews third-party documents, and approves or rejects. Approve and Reject exist only for third-party releases; normal releases are view-only here.
